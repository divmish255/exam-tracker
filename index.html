💡 What to Do When Panic Strikes During Preparation or Exam:

  • The 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Rule: Look around and spot 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, and 1 you taste. This immediately shuts down the amygdala fight-or-flight trigger.
  • Sip Cold Water: Activates the mammalian dive reflex, slowing heart rate within 20 seconds.
  • Skip and Return: In the exam hall, if question #1 or #2 looks foreign, skip to question #5 or #10. Build momentum with guaranteed marks first.
  • Never Study New Chapters at 2 AM: Sleep deprivation increases cortisol by 40%, guaranteeing memory fog the next morning.
